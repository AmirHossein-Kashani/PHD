## categories
- AI agents for algorithm discovery (e.g., AlphaEvolve-style systems).
- AI agents for generating hypotheses and answers (e.g., Kosmos- or AI coscientist–style systems).
- AI agents for automating scientific workflows and tasks (e.g., Virtual Lab–
  style systems).

## General section
**Axis 1 — What is being searched over?**
- *Procedure space:* code, algorithms, prompts, configurations
- *Concept space:* hypotheses, explanations, causal claims
- *Action space:* experimental protocols, tool sequences, lab procedures

**Axis 2 — How is the search controlled?**
- *Evolutionary/population-based:* maintain archives, mutate, select
- *Tournament/critique-based:* generate many, rank pairwise, refine winners
- *Gradient-like:* propagate structured feedback backward through a pipeline
- *Planner-driven:* a controller LLM decomposes goals and dispatches subtasks
- *Role-based/institutional:* mimic a research group with PI, specialists, critics
