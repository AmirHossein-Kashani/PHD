**1. ReAct** — Yao, S., Zhao, J., Yu, D., Du, N., Shafran, I., Narasimhan, K., & Cao, Y. (2023). *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models*. ICLR 2023.
  arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) — Interleaves reasoning traces and task-specific actions, with reasoning helping induce/track/update plans while actions interface with external sources like Wikipedia, ALFWorld, and WebShop. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
**2. Plan-and-Execute** — Wang, L., Xu, W., Lan, Y., Hu, Z., Lan, Y., Lee, R. K.-W., & Lim, E.-P. (2023). *Plan-and-Solve Prompting: Improving Zero-Shot Chain-of-Thought Reasoning by Large Language Models*. ACL 2023.
  arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04091](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04091) — proposes two components: first, devising a plan to divide the entire task into smaller subtasks, and then carrying out the subtasks according to the plan. The LangChain "Plan-and-Execute" agent is the direct implementation of this paper. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04091)
**3. Reflexion** — Shinn, N., Cassano, F., Berman, E., Gopinath, A., Narasimhan, K., & Yao, S. (2023). *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning*. NeurIPS 2023.
  arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366) — Reflexion agents verbally reflect on task feedback signals, then maintain their own reflective text in an episodic memory buffer to induce better decision-making in subsequent trials, exactly the "verbal reinforcement learning" mechanism in your table. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366)
**4. Tree of Thoughts / Graph of Thoughts** — two separate papers:
- Tree of Thoughts: Yao, S., Yu, D., Zhao, J., Shafran, I., Griffiths, T. L., Cao, Y., & Narasimhan, K. (2023). *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models*. NeurIPS 2023. arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601) — introduces exploration over coherent units of text (thoughts) that serve as intermediate steps, allowing LMs to consider multiple reasoning paths, self-evaluate, and look ahead or backtrack (i.e., the BFS/DFS + heuristic value function in the table). [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601)
- Graph of Thoughts: Besta, M., Blach, N., Kubicek, A., et al. (2024). *Graph of Thoughts: Solving Elaborate Problems with Large Language Models*. AAAI 2024. arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09687) — generalizes ToT by modeling information generated by an LLM as an arbitrary graph, where units of information ("LLM thoughts") are vertices, and edges correspond to dependencies between these vertices. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.09687)

**5. CodeAct** — Wang, X., Chen, Y., Yuan, L., Zhang, Y., Li, Y., Peng, H., & Ji, H. (2024). *Executable Code Actions Elicit Better LLM Agents*. ICML 2024.
  arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01030](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01030) — proposes using executable Python code to consolidate LLM agents' actions into a unified action space, integrated with a Python interpreter to execute code actions and dynamically revise prior actions or emit new actions upon new observations through multi-turn interactions. This is the paper your document is referencing when it contrasts JSON-based function calling against CodeAct. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01030)
**6. Multi-Agent Debate** — Du, Y., Li, S., Torralba, A., Tenenbaum, J. B., & Mordatch, I. (2023). *Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate*. ICML 2024.
  arXiv: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325) — multiple language model instances propose and debate their individual responses and reasoning processes over multiple rounds to arrive at a common final answer. A frequently cited companion paper is Liang et al. (2023), *Encouraging Divergent Thinking in Large Language Models through Multi-Agent Debate* (arXiv:2305.19118), which uses an explicit judge plus role personas closer to the "Scientist/Critic/Ontologist" framing in your document. [arXivarXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325)
